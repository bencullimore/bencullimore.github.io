Find out what your business needs to give the FCA

This service helps businesses understand what information they must provide to the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) to meet legal and regulatory requirements.

We'll ask about the services you offer and whether you're already authorised. You'll get a tailored list of what to submit to the FCA — including timings and estimated fees.

You can use this service if you:

  • are a UK-based business
  • offer financial services or services regulated under the Money Laundering Regulations
  • want to understand your regulatory obligations
