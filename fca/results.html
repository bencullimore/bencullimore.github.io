What you need to provide to the FCA

Follow these steps to understand what your business must give to the FCA — and when.

1 Apply to the FCA

You'll need to send information about your business before you can be authorised.

  • Tell us about your business model and activities
  • Tell us who your controllers and beneficial owners are
  • Submit your AML risk assessment and controls

Estimated application fee: £1,500 to £5,000 depending on what you do

Once you're authorised

2 Keep your details up to date

Tell the FCA about important changes to your business or key people.

  • Notify us of business changes
  • Update your AML-related contacts or structures

3 Submit annual returns

Each year you’ll need to report on your activity, risk, and finances.

  • Submit your financial crime report
  • Submit your revenue breakdown
  • Submit complaints data

Estimated annual costs:

  • Annual fee based on your firm’s income and services
  • Levy for each appointed representative, if you have any

If you plan to close your business

Let the FCA know if you plan to stop offering regulated services.

  • Start your wind-down process
